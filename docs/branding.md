# Rental Hunt KE - Branding Guide

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Approved
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# Brand Overview

**Rental Hunt KE** is a modern rental discovery platform designed to simplify house hunting in Kenya. The platform connects prospective tenants with verified rental listings through trusted agents, making the process transparent, efficient, and stress-free.

The brand is built around one central promise:

> **Trust first.**

Every product decision, design choice, and customer interaction should reinforce this promise.

---

# Brand Identity

## Brand Name

**Rental Hunt KE**

The name communicates exactly what the platform does while emphasizing its Kenyan focus. It is memorable, descriptive, and scalable as the business expands nationally.

---

## Category

Rental Discovery Platform

Rental Hunt KE is **not** a property management system and **not** a classifieds website. It is a platform focused on helping people discover, evaluate, and book viewings for rental properties.

---

## Tagline

**Find your next home with confidence.**

This tagline reflects the platform's core promise of trust and reliability.

---

# Mission

To make house hunting simple, transparent, and trustworthy by connecting people with verified rental listings and trusted local agents.

---

# Vision

To become Kenya's most trusted platform for discovering and securing rental homes.

---

# Brand Promise

Every listing presented on Rental Hunt KE should help users feel confident that:

* The property exists.
* The listing information is accurate.
* The assigned agent is legitimate.
* The viewing process is straightforward.
* Their time will not be wasted.

---

# Core Values

## Trust

Trust is the foundation of the platform.

Every listing, interaction, and feature should strengthen user confidence.

---

## Transparency

Provide clear, accurate, and up-to-date property information.

Avoid hidden costs, misleading descriptions, or deceptive marketing.

---

## Simplicity

House hunting is already complicated.

The platform should make every interaction straightforward and intuitive.

---

## Accuracy

Property information should remain current and verified.

Users should not encounter unavailable or misleading listings.

---

## Customer First

Every decision should prioritize helping renters find suitable homes quickly and confidently.

---

# Brand Personality

Rental Hunt KE should consistently feel:

* Professional
* Reliable
* Honest
* Helpful
* Modern
* Approachable
* Local
* Efficient

The platform should avoid appearing overly corporate, overly playful, or cluttered.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

* Young professionals
* Newly employed graduates
* Couples
* Families relocating
* Individuals moving within Nairobi and surrounding areas

## Secondary Audience

* Students
* Remote workers
* Expats
* Businesses assisting employee relocation

---

# Positioning Statement

Rental Hunt KE is the easiest and safest way to discover verified rental homes in Kenya.

Unlike informal marketplaces and social media listings, Rental Hunt KE prioritizes trust, verified information, and a professional viewing experience.

---

# Competitive Advantage

Rental Hunt KE differentiates itself through:

* Verified property listings
* Trusted local agents
* Current property availability
* High-quality listing information
* Seamless viewing requests
* A modern and intuitive user experience

The goal is not to have the largest number of listings but to provide the most trustworthy rental discovery experience.

---

# Brand Voice

Communication should always be:

* Clear
* Professional
* Friendly
* Helpful
* Honest
* Confident

Avoid:

* Clickbait language
* Exaggerated claims
* Pressure-selling
* Unnecessary jargon

Instead of saying:

> "Cheap apartments available!!!"

Prefer:

> "Discover verified rental homes that match your budget and lifestyle."

---

# Visual Direction

The interface should communicate trust, clarity, and professionalism.

## Design Principles

* Clean layouts
* Generous white space
* Consistent spacing
* Strong visual hierarchy
* High-quality photography
* Mobile-first design
* Fast-loading pages
* Accessible interfaces

The experience should feel closer to a modern technology platform than a traditional property classifieds website.

---

# Brand Colors

The visual identity should reinforce trust and simplicity.

### Primary

Deep Blue

Represents trust, professionalism, and stability.

### Secondary

Emerald Green

Represents successful home discovery, growth, and confidence.

### Neutral Palette

Soft grays and clean whites to maintain readability and reduce visual clutter.

---

# Typography

The typography should be modern, clean, and highly legible.

Preferred characteristics:

* Sans-serif
* Strong hierarchy
* Comfortable reading sizes
* Accessible contrast

Typography should support usability before aesthetics.

---

# Photography Style

Images should emphasize authenticity.

Preferred imagery includes:

* Bright, well-lit interiors
* Real residential spaces
* Minimal editing
* Wide-angle room photography
* Lifestyle images that feel natural

Avoid:

* Overly processed images
* Generic stock photography
* Misleading perspectives

---

# User Experience Principles

Every interaction should achieve one or more of the following:

* Build trust
* Save time
* Reduce uncertainty
* Help users make informed decisions
* Create confidence before scheduling a viewing

---

# Brand Success

A successful Rental Hunt KE experience is one where a user can confidently say:

> "I found exactly what I was looking for, and I trusted every step of the process."

When users associate Rental Hunt KE with reliability, transparency, and confidence, the brand has achieved its purpose.
