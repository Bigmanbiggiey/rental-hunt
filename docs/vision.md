# Rental Hunt KE - Product Vision

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Approved
> **Owner:** Product Team

---

# Vision Statement

To become Kenya's most trusted platform for discovering and securing rental homes by making the house-hunting experience transparent, efficient, and stress-free.

---

# Mission Statement

Rental Hunt KE exists to simplify the rental journey by connecting people with verified rental listings through trusted agents, enabling them to find their next home with confidence.

---

# Why We Exist

House hunting in Kenya is often time-consuming, frustrating, and risky.

Prospective tenants frequently encounter:

* Fake or misleading listings.
* Properties that are no longer available.
* Inconsistent pricing and information.
* Unresponsive or unreliable agents.
* Multiple unnecessary property visits.
* A fragmented search experience across social media, classifieds, and messaging platforms.

These challenges waste time, increase costs, and reduce trust in the rental market.

Rental Hunt KE exists to solve these problems by providing a trusted, centralized platform for rental discovery.

---

# The Problem We Are Solving

The current rental discovery process lacks transparency and consistency.

Renters struggle to determine whether:

* A listing is genuine.
* A property is still available.
* The advertised price is accurate.
* The agent is legitimate.
* The trip to a viewing is worthwhile.

This uncertainty creates unnecessary friction throughout the rental journey.

---

# Our Solution

Rental Hunt KE provides a modern rental discovery platform where users can:

* Discover verified rental properties.
* Search using meaningful filters.
* Explore detailed property information.
* View high-quality photos.
* Compare available options.
* Book physical property viewings through trusted agents.
* Make informed decisions before investing time and money.

The platform enhances—not replaces—the role of rental agents by making the discovery process more efficient for everyone involved.

---

# Product Vision

Rental Hunt KE aims to become the trusted bridge between renters and agents.

Rather than competing with agents, the platform empowers them by providing better tools for showcasing properties and managing prospective tenants.

Trust, accuracy, and simplicity are the defining characteristics of the product.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature developed for Rental Hunt KE should satisfy at least one of the following principles.

## Build Trust

Increase confidence in listings, agents, and information.

---

## Save Time

Reduce unnecessary property visits and simplify decision-making.

---

## Improve Transparency

Present accurate, complete, and up-to-date information.

---

## Empower Agents

Provide tools that help agents work more efficiently and professionally.

---

## Deliver a Better User Experience

Make house hunting easier, faster, and more enjoyable.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

* Young professionals
* Newly employed graduates
* Couples
* Families relocating within Kenya

## Secondary Audience

* Students
* Remote workers
* Expats
* Corporate relocation clients

The MVP will primarily target Nairobi before expanding to other regions.

---

# Success Metrics

The success of Rental Hunt KE will be measured using meaningful business outcomes rather than vanity metrics.

Key metrics include:

* Number of verified listings.
* Active monthly users.
* Viewing requests submitted.
* Viewing-to-rental conversion rate.
* Average response time to viewing requests.
* Listing freshness.
* Customer satisfaction.
* Returning users.
* Agent retention.

---

# Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

The MVP focuses on validating one core hypothesis:

> People are willing to use a trusted digital platform to discover rental properties and book physical viewings.

The MVP includes:

* Public property listings.
* Property search.
* Filtering.
* Property details.
* Image galleries.
* Agent profiles.
* Viewing request booking.
* Admin dashboard.
* Property management.
* Listing verification workflow.

---

# Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from the MVP:

* Online rent payments.
* Lease management.
* Tenant screening.
* Property maintenance management.
* Utility payments.
* Native mobile applications.
* AI-powered recommendations.
* Property sales.
* Landlord accounting.
* Property management operations.

These features may be evaluated after the core marketplace has been validated.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Rental Hunt KE aims to become the default destination for rental discovery in Kenya.

Future platform capabilities may include:

* Nationwide property coverage.
* Agency onboarding.
* Premium agency subscriptions.
* Featured listings.
* Market insights and analytics.
* Mobile applications.
* Smart property recommendations.
* Integration with digital payment platforms.
* Public APIs for ecosystem partners.

Growth will be guided by customer value rather than feature quantity.

---

# Definition of Success

Rental Hunt KE succeeds when renters no longer ask:

> "Is this listing genuine?"

Instead, they immediately trust that every property they discover through the platform is accurate, current, and professionally managed.

The platform will be recognized not for having the largest inventory, but for delivering the most trustworthy rental discovery experience in Kenya.
